A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Before you
get started.

Have an account question?
Your system administrator can help.

Who can use e-TAMBUA?+

e-TAMBUA is for authorized system administrators, watchlist managers and investigators. Your administrator provides your account and assigns your role.

Can I report a missing person here?+

This website introduces the platform. Case registration happens inside the authorized workspace; there is no public reporting form on this page.

How does photo search work?+

Authorized officers upload a photo. The face recognition engine compares it with the enrolled photos of active watchlist entries and open missing-person cases and returns the closest possible match, with its confidence. Every search is logged.

Are potential detections automatically confirmed?+

No. Authorized watchlist managers review the evidence and confirm or reject pending alerts and sightings. Confirmed entries can then appear in a subject’s movement timeline.
